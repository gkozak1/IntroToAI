Keep the lesson simple

  1. Network: click one neuron and calculate its weighted sum, bias, and activation.
  2. Layer: use Calculate Next Layer to reveal the rest only after the idea is clear.
  3. Matrix: convert one transition and see where every connection appears in the weight matrix.
  4. Dot Product: calculate one result entry and see that it is the same neuron calculation again.

The tool supports larger networks, but small examples are usually better for learning.
