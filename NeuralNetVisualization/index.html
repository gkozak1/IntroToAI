How the linked workspace works

  1. Click the z half of a neuron to focus on the weighted sum, bias, and pre-activation value.
  2. The same incoming weights are highlighted as a column in W, and the same result is highlighted in z.
  3. Click the a half to focus on activation and the value that moves forward to the next layer.
  4. Click an individual network edge, matrix weight, or dot-product term to trace that exact connection across all three views.

The network always uses two hidden layers and supports up to four neurons per layer.
